# Italian Cooking Basics

Italian cuisine is known for its simplicity and use of fresh, high-quality ingredients. The foundation of many dishes relies on olive oil, garlic, tomatoes, and fresh herbs.

## Pasta Techniques

Making fresh pasta requires just flour and eggs. The dough should be kneaded until smooth and elastic, then rested before rolling. Different shapes pair with different sauces.

## Classic Sauces

A proper marinara sauce starts with San Marzano tomatoes, garlic, and basil. Bolognese is a slow-cooked meat sauce from Bologna. Pesto originates from Genoa and combines basil, pine nuts, and Parmesan.
